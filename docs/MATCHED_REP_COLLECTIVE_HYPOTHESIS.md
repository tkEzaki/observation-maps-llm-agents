## Central claim

> Holding initial phases, natural frequencies, \(N\), \(K\), \(T\), system
> prompt, action set, and integrator fixed, intervening on the observation
> representation \(\mathcal R(\rho)\) changes macroscopic collective dynamics of

## Outcomes (preregistered)

### Outcome A — macroscopic transmutation

Same \(K\) and same physical initial condition yield systematically different
collective phenotypes across \(\mathcal R\), e.g.:

- moments → polar synchronization;
- centers → abstention / delayed ordering;
- intervals → multicluster / elevated higher harmonics.

At least one paired contrast (centers−moments or intervals−moments) on a
primary endpoint must exceed seed-block uncertainty with a coherent phenotype
story across \(K\).

### Outcome B — similar terminal state, different pathway

Final \(r_1\) may be similar, but **trajectory-level** quantities differ
systematically:

- mean activity;
- signed social torque;
- \(t_{0.5}\), \(t_{0.9}\);
- time-averaged / final \(r_2\), \(r_3\);
- collective frequency \(\Omega_{\mathrm{coll}}\).

Outcome B alone is scientifically interesting for the study if
pathway differences are robust and paired.

### Outcome null (NO-GO for the study macro claim)

- Representation contrasts fall inside seed variance;
- centers/intervals collectives indistinguishable from moments on all
  preregistered primary and pathway endpoints;
- cross-encoding replay later shows operator differences vanish on
  collective-generated fields (see protocol Phase-2 lock).

## the study submission gates

### GO

1. Matched direct collective comparison of three representations completed.
2. At least one robust macroscopic representation effect (Outcome A or B).
3. Cross-encoding replay mechanistic decomposition completed.
4. Second-model-family micro transmutation replication (conditional Phase R1).
5. Code, prompts, raw outputs, seeds, and hash manifests releasable.
6. Compressibility / transportability framework stated as general method.

### Borderline

- Macro comparison exists, but differences are activity / arrival-time only;
- no second-model replication.

Desk-rejection risk elevated; specialist venues remain viable.

### NO-GO (for the study macro narrative)

- Matched collectives ≈ moments across reps within seed noise;
- cross-encoding operator gaps disappear on endogenous fields.

Fallback: microscopic transmutation + \(\mathcal C\)/\(\mathcal T\) framework
to npj AI / Communications Physics / Phys. Rev. Research.
