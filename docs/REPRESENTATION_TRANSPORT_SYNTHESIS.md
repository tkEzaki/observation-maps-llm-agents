# Representation compressibility vs transportability — synthesis

Date: 2026-07-24  
Status: **locked cross-representation synthesis**  
Scope: moments / centers / intervals under tested serialization, descriptors, and label manifolds

## Central claim

> Microscopic representation effects and in-distribution predictability do not
> guarantee transportability of a response operator to an endogenous
> collective-field manifold.

This is an empirical result of the present study, not a claim that histogram
encodings are intrinsically unpredictable.

### Locked archive wording

**Centers.**  
Center-bin serialization induces reproducible microscopic transmutation and
peer-regime dependence, but its finite-peer response surface was not
sufficiently stable or compressible for prospective collective prediction
under the tested descriptors and coverage.

**Intervals.**  
Interval-bin serialization yields an in-distribution-compressible microscopic
response operator, but the available finite-peer training manifold does not
support reliable prospective transport to collective-like fields.

**Moments (Stage C v0.2 combined).**  
A replay-informed, regime-aware microscopic surrogate prospectively corrected
abstention bias and improved collective activity and stay predictions across
unseen \(K\), \(T\), \(N\), and \(\operatorname{sign}(K)\). Sync-speed /
\(K_c\) / torque precision and cross-representation macroscopic selection are
**not** claimed.

## 2. Methodological axes

### Compressibility \(\mathcal C\)

Improvement of a structured model over a **peer-specific global** baseline on
grouped / acquisition-block OOF, with **physical-field cluster bootstrap**
confidence intervals for \(\Delta\mathrm{LL}\).

### Transportability \(\mathcal T\)

Performance (or support-map viability) on fields generated by, or selected to
resemble, **endogenous collective dynamics** — prospective pilots, collective
replay, or locked support diagnoses — **not** the same Stage B label
manifold used for IID fit.

| Representation | \(\mathcal C\) | \(\mathcal T\) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moments | high | high after regime revision |
| Centers | high | low |
| Intervals | high | low |

**Implication.** Ordinary grouped CV alone would have incorrectly licensed
centers/intervals Stage C. Transport is a separate gate.

## 4. Paper-ready claim structure (scoped)

All claims are under tested serialization, descriptors, and label manifolds.

### Claim A — Representation transmutation

The same physical relative-phase field yields different trinomial response
operators under moments / intervals / centers serialization (Stage B
transmutation map; replicated).

### Claim B — Microscopic compressibility

Each representation’s response law is compressible on IID / grouped holdout
once peer regimes are stratified and an appropriate model family is used.

### Claim C — Transportability is representation-dependent

Compressibility does **not** imply transport to the endogenous collective-field
manifold. Centers and intervals fail this second gate; moments passes after
explicit regime revision informed by collective replay.

### Claim D — Moments is exceptional *in this study*

Only moments transported prospectively to unseen collective cells after a
replay-discovered regime boundary was built into the surrogate. This is **not**
a general superiority claim for moment encodings.

## 5. Failure-mode contrast (why intervals stop is hard)

Intervals support diagnoses (locked gate; peer16-only):

| diagnosis | ≈ fraction |
| --- | ---: |
| local_noncompressible | 0.64 |
| active_feature_gap | 0.30 |
| supported | 0.05 |

Nearest neighbors **exist**; neighbor **action distributions disagree**.
That is not a small-coverage hole fillable by a few more Stage-B-like labels.
Restart would require a **new research plan** (history / \(\Delta\rho\) /
new observables / collective-first acquisition / new prompt contract)—not
another INT-3 on the current manifold.

Centers failed with a related but distinct pattern: finite-peer abstention
phenotypes absent from training neighborhoods (feature-gap), plus weak
CENT-3 transfer despite strong OOF.

Moments’ repairable failure was **stay leakage across regimes**, fixed by a
descriptor-gated abstention/active split without inventing new physical
variables.
