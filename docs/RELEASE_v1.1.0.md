# Version 1.1.0 - September 22, 2026

This release retains the original 15 acquisition groups and adds:

- The September Claude concentration sweep: 25,920 accepted responses in two blocks, three encodings, five concentrations, 36 rotations, and 24 responses per cell.
- Complete attempt journals, accepted traces, stimuli, task orders, parser amendment and the separate original preflight journal. Duplicates and transport failures remain recorded but excluded from accepted-response counts.
- Acquisition, recovery, status and analysis scripts. Offline reproduction makes no provider calls.
- Frozen probability, coefficient and sensitivity tables, with an audit recomputing action probabilities and Fourier coefficients and checking prompt hashes.
- The exact 507,112-response count audit and a persistence check from existing trajectories.
- Current main-figure and Claude SI sources, historical SI modules, vector case-conversion inputs and scripts, and 44 current PDF assets.

The Claude follow-up was designed after the collective reversal was known and is descriptive. Model family and acquisition period were not independently randomized. Moment summaries versus histograms change both information and presentation; the two histograms retain the same bin masses. A positive directional coefficient alone is not a synchronization criterion.

The original v1.0.0 tag and MIT license are retained. Raw response strings and prompts are preserved. PDF rendering can vary with software/font versions; shipped artwork is the reference. The current driver redraws the five main figures and S27; historical SI uppercase conversions use frozen vector inputs. Original SI plotting sources are also supplied. The Claude integration module separately audits data and regenerates S26/S27 and sensitivity tables.

The release includes the final September 22 update to the Fig. 2 protocol schematic and its generating script. Shipped current artwork matches the manuscript figure directory byte for byte.
